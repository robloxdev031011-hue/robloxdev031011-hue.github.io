YouTube Multi Uploader

A personal, self-hosted tool for uploading videos to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

YouTube Multi Uploader is a private application that runs only on the owner's own computer. It lets the owner connect up to five of their own YouTube channels using Google's official sign-in (OAuth 2.0) and upload videos to them through the official YouTube Data API v3.

Google permissions used

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos the user chooses to their own channel.
  • youtube.readonly — to show the connected channel's name and picture.

The app never sees or stores Google passwords. It is not offered to the public.
