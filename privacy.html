Privacy Policy

YouTube Multi Uploader · Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who uses this app

YouTube Multi Uploader is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer (at http://localhost). It is used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

With your permission, through Google's official OAuth 2.0 sign-in, the app requests:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload videos that you select to your YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read your channel's ID, name and profile picture so the dashboard can show which channel is connected.

The app never sees, asks for, or stores your Google password.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted, in a local database.
  • Video files you upload are copied temporarily on the owner's computer and deleted after the upload finishes or fails.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube's official APIs.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than uploading your videos and showing your connected channel. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Click Disconnect in the app to delete a channel's stored tokens. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
